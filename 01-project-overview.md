# Project Overview — E-Commerce Checkout Improvement

## Project Background

An e-commerce company has identified several problems with its existing checkout experience.

Customers have reported difficulty managing payment methods, unclear payment-error messages, and a lack of flexibility when entering shipping information.

The Product Owner has established a goal of improving the checkout experience through incremental delivery.

The Scrum Team will work through three Sprints to deliver these improvements.

---

## Product Goal

Improve the customer checkout experience by making payment, shipping, and order confirmation simpler, clearer, and more reliable.

---

## Scrum Team

### Product Owner

Responsible for maximizing product value and managing Product Backlog priorities.

### Scrum Master

Responsible for facilitating Scrum, helping the team understand and apply Scrum, supporting effective Scrum events, identifying impediments, and helping the team continuously improve.

### Developers

A simulated cross-functional development team responsible for creating the product Increment during each Sprint.

The Developers determine how the work is technically implemented.

---

## Scrum Framework

The project follows Scrum using:

* Product Backlog
* Sprint Backlog
* Sprint Goal
* User Stories
* Acceptance Criteria
* Story Points
* Daily Scrum
* Sprint Review
* Sprint Retrospective
* Definition of Done
* Incremental delivery
* Continuous improvement

---

## Project Duration

The project consists of three simulated Sprints.

**Sprint Length:** 2 weeks

### Sprint 1

**Focus:** Core checkout improvements

### Sprint 2

**Focus:** Payment and customer experience improvements

### Sprint 3

**Focus:** Final checkout improvements and optimization

---

## Initial Business Requirements

The Product Owner has identified the following desired improvements:

1. Allow customers to save payment methods.
2. Improve payment failure messaging.
3. Allow customers to edit their shipping address during checkout.
4. Provide digital payment options.
5. Improve order confirmation.
6. Improve the mobile checkout experience.
7. Reduce customer confusion during payment.

These requirements will be converted into User Stories and managed through the Product Backlog.

---

## Scrum Master's Focus

Throughout the project, the Scrum Master's focus will be on enabling the Scrum Team to work effectively rather than directing how Developers perform technical work.

Key areas of focus include:

* Facilitating Scrum events
* Helping the team establish effective Sprint Goals
* Supporting Product Backlog refinement
* Helping identify impediments
* Facilitating resolution of impediments
* Encouraging transparency
* Supporting collaboration between the Product Owner and Developers
* Helping the team inspect its progress
* Facilitating Retrospectives
* Supporting continuous improvement

---

## Definition of Done

A User Story is considered Done when:

* The functionality has been implemented.
* Required code has been reviewed.
* Required testing has been completed.
* Acceptance criteria have been satisfied.
* No known critical defects remain.
* The functionality is integrated into the product Increment.
* The team agrees that the work meets the Definition of Done.

---

## Project Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when the Scrum Team:

* Delivers usable product increments during the Sprints.
* Understands and works toward clear Sprint Goals.
* Maintains a transparent Product Backlog.
* Identifies and addresses impediments.
* Uses Sprint Reviews to inspect the Increment and gather feedback.
* Uses Retrospectives to identify meaningful improvements.
* Demonstrates measurable improvement in the team's way of working across the three Sprints.

---

## Portfolio Objective

This project is designed to demonstrate practical Scrum Master capabilities through a realistic simulated environment.

The emphasis is on facilitation, transparency, impediment management, inspection, adaptation, and continuous improvement rather than software development.
