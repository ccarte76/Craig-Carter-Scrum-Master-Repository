# Sprint 1 Planning

## Sprint Information

**Sprint:** Sprint 1
**Sprint Length:** 2 weeks
**Sprint Goal:** Improve the core checkout experience by making order review, payment failure handling, and order confirmation clearer and more reliable.

---

## Sprint Planning Objective

The purpose of Sprint Planning is to establish:

1. Why the Sprint is valuable.
2. What work can be completed during the Sprint.
3. How the selected work will be delivered.

The Scrum Team reviewed the Product Backlog and discussed the highest-priority items that could contribute to the Sprint Goal.

---

## Scrum Master Facilitation

As Scrum Master, I facilitated the Sprint Planning discussion and supported the Scrum Team in:

* Understanding the Sprint Goal.
* Clarifying User Stories.
* Discussing acceptance criteria.
* Identifying dependencies.
* Reviewing story-point estimates.
* Confirming that selected work supported the Sprint Goal.
* Ensuring the team established a realistic Sprint Backlog.
* Encouraging the Developers to determine how the work would be completed.

The Developers remained responsible for determining how the selected work would be technically implemented.

# Sprint Goal

> **Improve the core checkout experience by making order review, payment failure handling, and order confirmation clearer and more reliable.**

# Why These Stories Were Selected

The selected stories directly support the Sprint Goal by improving transparency and reducing confusion during checkout.

The team discussed the relationship between the stories and identified that several of them could be developed together as part of the checkout experience.

The team also reviewed acceptance criteria and discussed whether any stories required additional clarification before development began.

---

# Sprint Planning Discussion

### Product Owner Perspective

The Product Owner emphasized that customers needed clearer information before and after payment.

The highest-value improvements identified for the Sprint were:

* Order confirmation
* Payment failure messaging
* Order review
* Visibility of taxes and shipping costs
* Clear checkout error messages

### Developers' Perspective

The Developers reviewed the stories and their acceptance criteria.

The Developers confirmed that the selected stories were sufficiently understood to begin the Sprint while identifying potential dependencies that should be monitored.

The Developers retained responsibility for determining the technical approach.

### Scrum Master Perspective

The Scrum Master's focus was on facilitating the conversation and ensuring:

* The Sprint Goal was clear.
* The selected stories supported the Sprint Goal.
* The team understood the work.
* Dependencies and potential impediments were discussed.
* The Sprint Backlog represented work the Developers believed they could realistically undertake.
* The team understood the outcome they were working toward.

---

# Initial Impediments and Risks

The team identified the following potential risks before beginning the Sprint:

### Risk 1 — Payment System Dependency

The payment failure functionality may depend on information from an external payment service.

**Action:** Track the dependency during the Sprint and identify any blockage early.

### Risk 2 — Story Clarification

Some checkout error scenarios may require additional clarification.

**Action:** Product Owner and Developers will clarify acceptance criteria as needed.

# Definition of Done

The team will use the following Definition of Done for Sprint 1:

* Functionality has been implemented.
* Acceptance criteria have been satisfied.
* Required testing has been completed.
* Code review has been completed where applicable.
* No known critical defects remain.
* The functionality is integrated into the product Increment.
* The team agrees the work meets the Definition of Done.

---

# Sprint 1 Expected Outcome

At the end of the Sprint, the team expects to have a usable Increment containing improvements to:

* Order confirmation
* Payment failure messaging
* Order review
* Cost transparency
* Checkout error communication

The Sprint Review will be used to inspect the resulting Increment and gather feedback.

The Sprint Retrospective will be used to inspect the team's way of working and identify improvements for the next Sprint.
