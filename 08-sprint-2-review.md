# Sprint 2 Review

## Sprint

**Sprint 2**

**Sprint Goal:**
Improve the checkout experience by expanding payment options and giving customers greater control and visibility over shipping.

## Sprint Outcome

The team completed 18 story points during Sprint 2.

| User Story                     | Result        | Points |
| ------------------------------ | ------------- | -----: |
| US-04 — Digital Payment Option | Completed     |      8 |
| US-18 — Shipping Options       | Completed     |      5 |
| US-03 — Edit Shipping Address  | Completed     |      5 |
| US-12 — Estimated Delivery     | Not Completed |      5 |

**Completed:** 18 points

## Product Increment

The completed work improved the checkout experience by:

* Adding a digital payment option.
* Providing customers with additional shipping choices.
* Allowing customers to edit their shipping address.

US-12 — Estimated Delivery was not completed because of an external API dependency.

## US-12 Discussion

The team explained that the shipping provider's API did not provide the required information in the expected format.

The provider indicated that its engineering team would not be available for approximately three business days.

Because the dependency was unresolved, the team could not provide a reliable development completion estimate.

The Scrum Master communicated this information transparently to the Product Owner and stakeholders.

US-12 was returned to the Product Backlog for consideration in a future Sprint.

## Stakeholder Feedback

### Marketing

Marketing reported that customers were asking for estimated delivery information and considered the feature valuable.

### Operations

Operations requested that the team consider an order-tracking capability as a future improvement.

### Product Owner

Based on stakeholder feedback, the Product Owner decided that US-12 should remain a high-priority Product Backlog Item.

The Product Owner also requested that the team investigate an order-tracking capability for a future Sprint.

## Scrum Master Facilitation

The Scrum Master facilitated discussion between the Product Owner, Developers, and stakeholders.

The Scrum Master:

* Made the US-12 dependency transparent.
* Communicated the impact of the unresolved dependency.
* Facilitated stakeholder feedback.
* Supported the Product Owner in gathering information for Product Backlog prioritization.
* Avoided making Product Backlog priority decisions on behalf of the Product Owner.
* Avoided directing Developers' technical work.

## Follow-Up Actions

### US-12 — Estimated Delivery

The dependency will continue to be monitored.

The team will clarify the technical requirements and dependency during future backlog refinement.

### Order Tracking

The Product Owner requested that an order-tracking capability be investigated as a potential future Product Backlog Item.

The Developers will provide technical input during refinement.

## Scrum Master Reflection

The Sprint Review demonstrated the importance of transparency when a Sprint Backlog Item is not completed.

Rather than providing an unsupported completion date, the Scrum Master communicated the known dependency and its impact.

The Review also provided an opportunity for stakeholders to provide feedback that could influence future Product Backlog priorities.

The Scrum Master's role was to facilitate the conversation and ensure the Product Owner and Developers had the information needed to make decisions.
