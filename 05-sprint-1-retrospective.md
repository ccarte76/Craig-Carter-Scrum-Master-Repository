# Sprint 1 Retrospective

## Sprint

**Sprint 1**

**Duration:** 2 weeks

**Sprint Goal:**
Improve the core checkout experience by making order review, payment failure handling, and order confirmation clearer and more reliable.

## Sprint Outcome

The team completed 4 of the 5 planned user stories.

| User Story                      | Result        |
| ------------------------------- | ------------- |
| US-02 — Order Confirmation      | Completed     |
| US-05 — Payment Failure Message | Completed     |
| US-07 — Order Review            | Completed     |
| US-09 — Taxes & Shipping Costs  | Not Completed |
| US-14 — Checkout Error Messages | Completed     |

**Completed:** 16 story points
**Planned:** 21 story points

US-09 was not completed during the Sprint and remained in the Product Backlog for future consideration.

## Retrospective Discussion

### What Went Well

* The payment environment access impediment was identified and actively addressed.
* The team completed the Sprint Goal-related work that was prioritized for the Sprint.
* Requirement clarification was addressed when Developers identified ambiguity.
* The team maintained communication with the Product Owner when issues affected progress.

### What Did Not Go Well

The team identified two significant sources of delay:

1. Requirements for US-07 were not sufficiently clear before development began.
2. Access to the payment test environment was not available when needed.

These issues created avoidable delays during the Sprint.

## Scrum Master Observation

The discussion showed that some Sprint delays could have been reduced through earlier clarification of requirements and identification of dependencies.

The focus should not simply be on increasing development speed. The team should improve the conditions that allow Developers to work effectively.

## Improvement Action

### Action: Improve Backlog Refinement

For the next Sprint, the team will place greater emphasis on:

* Clarifying acceptance criteria before Sprint Planning.
* Identifying external dependencies earlier.
* Discussing questions raised by Developers before Sprint work begins.
* Confirming that selected Product Backlog Items meet the team's Definition of Ready.

### Owner

**Scrum Master:** Facilitate the improvement.

**Product Owner:** Provide clarification on product requirements.

**Developers:** Identify technical questions, dependencies, and risks during refinement.

## Success Measure

During Sprint 2, the team will track whether unclear requirements or unresolved dependencies cause significant development delays.

The goal is to reduce avoidable rework and waiting time while maintaining focus on the Sprint Goal.

## Scrum Master Reflection

This Sprint demonstrated that impediments are not always technical problems. Requirement ambiguity and missing access can also prevent a Scrum Team from making effective progress.

The Scrum Master's role is to facilitate transparency, help the team identify obstacles, and support continuous improvement rather than directing Developers' technical work.
