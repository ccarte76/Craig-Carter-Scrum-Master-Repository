# Sprint 2 Planning

## Sprint

**Sprint 2**

**Duration:** 2 weeks

## Sprint Goal

> Improve the checkout experience by expanding payment options and giving customers greater control and visibility over shipping.

## Sprint Planning Discussion

The Product Owner identified two high-priority business items:

* US-04 — Digital Payment Option
* US-06 — Mobile Checkout

The Developers raised concerns about taking both items because each was estimated at 8 story points.

The team also identified a dependency affecting US-06. The mobile payment component required for the story was not currently accessible, and the timeline for obtaining access was unclear.

### Scrum Master Facilitation

The Scrum Master facilitated discussion between the Product Owner and Developers rather than deciding the Sprint Backlog independently.

The team considered:

* Product priority
* Developer capacity
* Story complexity
* Dependencies
* Ability to realistically complete the work during the Sprint

Because US-06 had an unresolved dependency, the team decided not to include it in Sprint 2.

The Developers indicated that they were comfortable taking US-04 plus three additional 5-point stories.

## Product Owner Priorities

The Product Owner provided the following priority order for the remaining 5-point stories:

1. US-18 — Shipping Options
2. US-03 — Edit Shipping Address
3. US-12 — Estimated Delivery
4. US-17 — Email Receipt

Based on the Product Owner's priorities and the Developers' assessment, the team selected US-18, US-03, and US-12.

## Sprint Backlog

| ---------- | ---------------------- | -----------: |
| US-04      | Digital Payment Option |            8 |
| US-18      | Shipping Options       |            5 |

## Why US-06 Was Not Selected

US-06 — Mobile Checkout was not selected because it had an unresolved dependency involving access to a required mobile payment component.

The team chose not to commit to work where a significant dependency remained unresolved.

The dependency will be monitored and addressed so the story can be reconsidered in a future Sprint.

## Scrum Master Responsibilities

During Sprint Planning, the Scrum Master focused on:

* Facilitating discussion between the Product Owner and Developers.
* Making the dependency on US-06 visible.
* Ensuring the Developers had an opportunity to assess their capacity.
* Helping the team reach a realistic Sprint commitment.
* Supporting the Product Owner in communicating priorities.
* Avoiding directing Developers on how the technical work should be completed.

## Definition of Done

Sprint work will be considered complete when the agreed acceptance criteria are satisfied and the work meets the team's Definition of Done.

## Sprint Risks

### US-06 Dependency

The mobile payment component remains an unresolved dependency.

**Action:** Track the dependency and coordinate with the appropriate owner to determine when access can be provided.

### Sprint Scope

The team selected 23 story points based on its assessment of what is realistically achievable.

**Action:** Monitor progress throughout the Sprint and make the Sprint Goal and progress toward it transparent.

## Expected Outcome

By the end of Sprint 2, the team aims to improve the checkout experience by:

* Providing an additional digital payment option.
* Giving customers more shipping choices.
* Allowing customers to edit their shipping address.
* Providing estimated delivery information.
