# Sprint 1 — Impediment Management

## Impediment #001 — Payment Service Dependency

**Sprint:** Sprint 1
**Date Identified:** Day 3
**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** Resolved

---

## Situation

During the Sprint, the Developers identified an issue affecting the implementation and testing of the payment failure functionality.

The team needed access to information from the external payment service in order to properly test several payment-failure scenarios.

The required information was not immediately available to the team.

This created a potential risk to the Sprint Goal.

---

## Impact

The impediment affected:

* US-05 — Payment Failure Message
* US-14 — Clear Checkout Error Messages

Without access to the required payment-service information, the Developers could not fully validate the expected behavior for certain payment failure scenarios.

---

## Scrum Master's Response

The Scrum Master's responsibility was not to solve the technical problem personally.

Instead, I focused on helping the team make the impediment visible and facilitating its resolution.

### Actions Taken

1. Confirmed the nature and impact of the impediment with the Developers.
2. Documented the impediment so it remained visible.
3. Identified the external dependency responsible for the blockage.
4. Facilitated communication between the Developers and the relevant dependency owner.
5. Confirmed what information was required to unblock the team.
6. Followed up on the dependency rather than allowing it to remain unresolved.
7. Communicated the status of the impediment to the relevant stakeholders.
8. Confirmed with the Developers when the required information became available.

---

## Resolution

The required payment-service information was provided to the Developers.

The Developers were then able to continue testing the affected functionality.

The impediment was marked **Resolved**.

---

## Impact on Sprint

The team was able to continue working toward the Sprint Goal.

The impediment did not require removal of the affected User Stories from the Sprint Backlog.

The team continued monitoring the dependency until the affected work was completed.

# What This Demonstrates

This situation demonstrates the Scrum Master's role in:

* Making impediments visible
* Facilitating communication
* Supporting the Developers
* Removing obstacles to progress
* Monitoring dependencies
* Protecting the team's ability to focus on the Sprint Goal
* Avoiding taking ownership of technical implementation

The Scrum Master did not determine the technical solution. The Developers remained responsible for the technical implementation and testing.

---

# Scrum Master Reflection

The impediment highlighted the importance of identifying external dependencies early.

For future Sprints, the team can improve by discussing external dependencies during Product Backlog refinement and Sprint Planning.

A useful question during refinement will be:

> **"Does completing this User Story depend on another team, system, vendor, or external service?"**

This can help the team identify potential blockers before the Sprint begins.
