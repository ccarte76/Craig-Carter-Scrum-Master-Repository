# Sprint 2 Execution & Impediment

## Sprint

**Sprint 2**

**Sprint Goal:**
Improve the checkout experience by expanding payment options and giving customers greater control and visibility over shipping.

## Day 4 — Impediment

During the Daily Scrum, James identified an issue with the shipping provider's API.

The API was not returning estimated delivery dates in the format the team expected. This affected **US-12 — Estimated Delivery**.

### Team Situation

**Mike:**
Completed the digital payment story and began working on shipping options.

**James:**
Was working on shipping options and identified the API format issue.

**Alex:**
Was working on the address editing story and reported no blockers.

**Product Owner:**
Confirmed that estimated delivery information was important to the Sprint.

## Scrum Master Response

The Scrum Master identified the API issue as an external dependency and began facilitating communication with the shipping provider.

The Scrum Master:

* Identified the external dependency.
* Communicated the issue to the appropriate external party.
* Asked for clarification regarding the API response format.
* Made the impediment visible to the Scrum Team.
* Communicated the potential Sprint impact to the Product Owner.
* Continued monitoring the dependency.

The Scrum Master did not direct the Developers' technical implementation.

## External Dependency Response

The shipping provider responded that the requested information could be provided, but their engineering team would not be available for another three business days.

This created a potential delay to **US-12 — Estimated Delivery**.

## Product Owner Discussion

The Product Owner initially wanted the team to continue with US-12 because the feature was important.

The Scrum Master explained the dependency and its potential impact.

The Scrum Master asked the Product Owner whether US-12 could be deferred to a future Sprint if the dependency could not be resolved in time.

The Product Owner agreed that, if necessary, US-12 could be moved to the next Sprint so the team could continue making progress toward the Sprint Goal.

## Decision

The team agreed to:

* Track the external API dependency.
* Continue working on other Sprint Backlog Items.
* Avoid having the Developers remain blocked unnecessarily.
* Reassess US-12 once the required API information becomes available.
* Maintain transparency with the Product Owner regarding the impact.

The Scrum Master did not independently decide what the Developers should work on next.

## Impediment Log

| Impediment                      | Impact                    | Action                                | Status     |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Shipping API format unavailable | US-12 potentially delayed | Contact provider and track dependency | Monitoring |

## Scrum Master Reflection

This situation demonstrated the importance of identifying and managing external dependencies during Sprint execution.

The Scrum Master helped facilitate resolution while allowing the Developers to determine how to organize their work.

When the dependency could not immediately be resolved, the Scrum Master worked with the Product Owner to evaluate whether the affected Product Backlog Item should remain in the Sprint.

The focus remained on transparency, impediment removal, and progress toward the Sprint Goal.
